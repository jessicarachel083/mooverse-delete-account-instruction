MOOverse - Penghapusan Akun

Halaman ini menjelaskan cara pengguna aplikasi MOOverse untuk meminta penghapusan akun dan data terkait.

Cara Menghapus Akun

Untuk menghapus akun pada aplikasi MOOverse:

  1. Masuk (login) ke aplikasi MOOverse
  2. Buka tab Profile
  3. Pilih menu Account Details
  4. Klik tombol Delete Account
  5. Pada konfirmasi yang muncul, klik Delete
  6. Jika ingin membatalkan, klik Cancel

Data yang Dihapus

Saat akun dihapus, data berikut akan dihapus dari sistem kami:

  • Data membership pengguna
  • Status loyalitas dan poin pengguna
  • Data akun terkait aplikasi MOOverse

Data yang Tetap Disimpan

Beberapa data tetap disimpan untuk kebutuhan administratif, keamanan, audit, dan riwayat transaksi.

Data transaksi pembelian dapat tetap disimpan pada sistem kami sesuai kebutuhan operasional dan ketentuan hukum yang berlaku.

MOOverse - Account Deletion

This page explains how users of the MOOverse application can request account deletion and related data removal.

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your MOOverse account:

  1. Log in to the MOOverse application
  2. Open the Profile tab
  3. Select the Account Details menu
  4. Tap the Delete Account button
  5. On the confirmation dialog, tap Delete
  6. To cancel the process, tap Cancel

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be removed from our system:

  • User membership data
  • Loyalty status and reward points
  • Account-related data connected to MOOverse

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may still be retained for administrative, security, audit, and transaction history purposes.

Purchase transaction records may remain stored in accordance with operational requirements and applicable laws.
